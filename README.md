# SheSolves Nexus

Build an Official ACM-W PCCOE Website for “SheSolves 2026”

Create a premium, modern, responsive official event landing page for:

Event: SheSolves 2026
Tagline: Code. Create. Empower.
Organizer: PCET’s Pimpri Chinchwad College of Engineering (PCCOE), Department of Computer Engineering
Chapter: ACM-W Student Chapter, PCCOE
Event Type: Women-Only Hackathon

The website should feel like a real professional ACM-W hackathon website, not a basic college project.

1. FIRST UNDERSTAND THE EVENT

Before designing the website, understand what She Solves represents.

She Solves is a women-only technology hackathon organized by PCCOE's Department of Computer Engineering / ACM-W ecosystem. Its purpose is to encourage women students to:

identify real-world problems

develop innovative technology-based solutions

collaborate in teams

improve technical and problem-solving skills

present and defend their projects

gain confidence and leadership experience

build solutions with meaningful social impact

Previous She Solves editions followed a multi-stage hackathon format involving ideation/proposal submission, online development, offline development/final coding, and project presentation/judging.

The 2026 website should present SheSolves as an evolving flagship ACM-W event, combining technology, creativity, collaboration, innovation and women empowerment.

Do NOT describe it as a generic women's conference or seminar.

It is primarily a hackathon / innovation competition.
